05FAQ

Straight answers.

How fast can a campaign go live?+
Three working days from creative approval. The media plan itself takes one call and one draft.
Is there a minimum spend?+
The floor is the R1,500 management minimum plus your media budget. Campaigns genuinely work from about R5,000 in media, and I will tell you honestly if yours is too small to pay for itself.
Do I need my own creative?+
If you have it, great. If not, I spec exactly what is needed per channel and can point you to designers who work small budgets. Creative requirements are in the plan before you commit.
Am I locked in?+
No. Month to month, pause or stop whenever you like. The media plan is yours to take anywhere, even if you leave.
What if a channel does not perform?+
Budget moves to one that does, the same week the data says so. No channel gets kept alive on hope, and you see the reasoning in every report.
