07FAQ

Straight answers.

What exactly is a proof of play?+
The receipt for an ad play: a photo of your creative on the screen it ran on, the exact timestamp, the board location, and the impressions that play earned. For digital channels the equivalent is verified impression and click logs from the buying platform.
Do I get proofs for online campaigns too?+
Yes. Outdoor plays are proven with photos and timestamps. Digital campaigns are proven with platform-level logs: impressions, clicks, views and costs, exportable and verifiable. Same principle: you never trust, you verify.
Is there a minimum spend?+
The floor is the R1,500 management minimum plus your media budget. Campaigns genuinely work from about R5,000 in media, and I will tell you honestly if yours is too small to pay for itself.
How fast can a campaign go live?+
Three working days from creative approval. The media plan itself takes one call and one draft.
Am I locked in?+
No. Month to month, pause or stop whenever you like. The media plan and every proof of play are yours to keep, even if you leave.
